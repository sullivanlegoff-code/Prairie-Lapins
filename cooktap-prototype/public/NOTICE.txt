The earlier prototype adapted pizza/pasta recipe data and a timing-based score
rule. The current landscape kitchen foundation replaces that gameplay with
locally written object state, geometry, Pointer Events and provisional SVG art.
No upstream gameplay classes, images, fonts, audio or external assets are loaded.
